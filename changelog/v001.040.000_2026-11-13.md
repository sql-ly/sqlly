# 2026-11-13

## Theme choices grouped in one View ▸ Theme submenu

The four loose "Theme: …" items at the bottom of the View menu are now one Theme submenu with System, Light, Dark and High Contrast, and a checkmark shows which mode you're on and whether High Contrast is active. Clicking a checked High Contrast (or running Theme: High Contrast from the command palette) now turns it off again, back to the default palette for the mode you're in; a High Contrast palette you picked in Settings for the other mode is left alone. The checks stay in step when you change the theme from Settings, and the in-window menu bar on Windows and Linux shows them too. Suggested by Josue and Jeffry.

## Locate interface text in the translation preview

Selecting a translation in the translation manager outlines where its text appears in the live preview, and Pick from preview opens the translation for any text you click there. When several translations share the same wording, the outline is dashed and the pick lists all of them so you can choose. Pick mode shows a crosshair, ignores text hidden behind dialogs, and Escape leaves it from either the preview or the page. The translated text itself is never altered, so inputs, copies and sorting behave exactly as they do without it.

## Japanese, Korean, Traditional Chinese and Russian render in the browser build

The browser build drew most of the Japanese, Korean, Traditional Chinese and Russian interface as empty boxes: it has no system fonts, and its built-in fonts covered only Latin and common Simplified Chinese. Russian now uses a built-in Noto Sans that includes Cyrillic and Greek. Japanese, Korean and Traditional Chinese each load their own Noto Sans face the first time you switch to that language (about 2–2.5 MB, cached afterwards), so shared characters take the right regional shapes, and until it arrives the browser's own fonts fill in instead of boxes. Characters outside the bundled sets, such as rare ideographs in query results or a translator's preview, also fall back to the browser's fonts. Seven Simplified Chinese characters the interface uses were missing too and are now included, and a German label stored with a decomposed umlaut is fixed.
