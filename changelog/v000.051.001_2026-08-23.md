# 2026-08-23

## Dependency advisory and new-toolchain lint fixes

The `h2` HTTP/2 dependency was raised to 0.4.18 to clear RUSTSEC-2026-0258 (unbounded empty DATA frames, low severity), and three byte-decoding helpers now carry an explicit allowance for clippy 1.98's new `chunks_exact` lint, whose suggested replacement needs a newer Rust than the workspace's declared minimum.
