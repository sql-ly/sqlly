# 2026-09-19

## Migrated to GPUI Kit (gpui-component 0.6, registry-only toolkit)

The UI toolkit moved to the reorganized GPUI Kit distribution: the app and the sqlly-datatable grid (new v6.0.0 tag) now build against crates.io releases of gpui-component 0.6.4 and the gpui-pre 0.3.5 publication of the current gpui API, with icons from gpui-kit-assets and the platform layer (including the browser's WebAssembly backend) from gpui-pre-platform. All the zed and longbridge git patch workarounds are gone from both workspaces, so builds are reproducible from the registry alone and the datatable can be published to crates.io again. No user-visible behavior changes; the full test suites, the wasm browser bundle, and a sandboxed app launch were verified on the new toolkit.
