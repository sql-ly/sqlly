# 2026-10-22

## The results grid's right-click menu is grouped into submenus

The results grid's right-click menu had grown to 34 to 55 entries. It now keeps the everyday actions at the top (Copy, Copy with Headers, Select All, the cell's viewer, Open Referenced Row, the most common Add WHERE, Export…) and files the rest in submenus that open beside the row, by hovering, clicking, or with the arrow keys: Copy As, Filter, Edit Cell, Format (with Masking and Rounding inside), and Tools (Find & Replace, Client Statistics, Transpose, Chart, Open in Spreadsheet, Save as Snapshot, Layout). Right-clicking a column header gives Copy column, Sort Ascending and Descending, Filter..., and the Sort & Filter, Column, Values, and Format submenus. Row headers get a Row submenu for duplicating, inserting, and staging edits. Every menu now has at most 12 entries in at most 4 groups, and nothing was removed.
