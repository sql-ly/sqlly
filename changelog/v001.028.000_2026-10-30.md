# 2026-10-30

## Standalone builds

SQLly can now be built as a fully standalone app that never contacts sqlly.app. In a standalone build, account sign-in, license checks and reminders, feedback, update checks and Cloud Relay are switched off, and each one says so plainly when you reach for it instead of failing quietly. Everything that works locally (connections, the editor, IntelliSense, results, exports, your own AI providers) is unchanged.

## Feedback only from official builds

Send Feedback and Improve IntelliSense (F2) now work only in official SQLly builds. A build compiled from source tells you feedback is disabled instead of trying to send a report that would be refused.

## Feedback uploads stop retrying when they can never succeed

Feedback that's waiting to upload no longer retries every five minutes forever no matter what went wrong. A report sqlly.app will never accept (for example, one that's too large) is discarded and the status bar says why. When a rate limit is hit, the status bar says so and when the report will be sent, and the app waits until then before trying again. Reports still survive network outages and restarts as before.
