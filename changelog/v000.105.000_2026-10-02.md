# 2026-10-02

## SQLLY is ready for the Mac App Store

There is now a Mac App Store edition of SQLLY, built from the same source as the direct download. It's a free download that saves up to two connections, and a one-time $150 in-app purchase through your Apple account unlocks unlimited connections and turns off the reminders. SQLLY → Unlock SQLLY… shows the App Store price and has Restore Purchases for a new Mac or a reinstall, and a license bought on sqlly.app also unlocks it once you sign in. In this edition the App Store handles updates, files and folders you pick (database files, SSH keys, project folders) stay available after a relaunch, and the Postgres and MySQL dump and restore tools come built in. Opening a terminal, local AI agent command-line tools, local MCP servers, Kubernetes discovery, command-line editors, git integration and downloading DuckDB extensions aren't available there, because the App Store sandbox doesn't allow launching other programs or downloading code. Each of these says so where you'd use it, Help → Mac App Store Edition lists every difference (it also opens once on first launch). Query-finished alerts use macOS notifications, Help → Privacy Policy and the Account window link to the privacy policy, and the Account window has Delete Account… for removing your SQLLY account. The direct-download edition is unchanged.
