## Use SQLLY in English or Spanish

The entire application is translated in English and Spanish, including menus, Settings, dialogs, connection tools, results, charts, AI features, administration, shared grids, and browser chrome. Choose a native-language name under Settings → Appearance → Language and apply it immediately, or supply a custom JSON file to override selected wording. Browser hosts can also force language and catalog updates live without changing the user's saved preferences.
