# 2026-11-24

## Enter after an open parenthesis no longer gets stuck

With "format as you go" on, pressing Enter after the `(` of a `CREATE TABLE` (or any statement with a parenthesis still open) reformatted the statement and put the caret back in front of the `(`. Each Enter opened a new line above the paren, so you could never get to the line below it. Format as you go now leaves a statement alone while a parenthesis is unclosed, so Enter is a plain newline. It also keeps the caret after parentheses you typed (`count(*)|`) when it does reformat. New editor tests type whole `CREATE TABLE` statements one key at a time and check the caret after every key, with the setting on and off.
