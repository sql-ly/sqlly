# SQLly commercial license

If your use isn't covered by the PolyForm Small Business License or the additional grant in [`LICENSING.md`](LICENSING.md), you need a commercial license.

- **What counts as a commercial license:** any paid SQLly plan, whether bought at <https://sqlly.app/pricing>, through the Mac App Store, or through the Microsoft Store.
- **Terms:** the commercial terms are at <https://sqlly.app/terms>. They apply to the official builds and to builds you make yourself from this source.
- **Evaluation:** a company that doesn't qualify for the free license may evaluate SQLly for 30 days before buying.
- **Standalone builds:** building with the `standalone` feature turns off the app's license checks. It doesn't change what license you need.

For volume, procurement or custom terms, email <support@sqlly.app>.
